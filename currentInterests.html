About Michael Earl's Personal Interests

I'm a software engineer with a background primarily in backend systems. I'm drawn to risk and stretch assignments — failing fast on side projects so I don't fail slow at work.

Outside of work I do ballroom dancing, play guitar, play soccer, and box. None of these am I great at, which is the point. Staying a beginner at things keeps the learning mindset sharp. I'm also a committed OU football fan — it's my alma mater.

I built this site deliberately. My day-to-day lives deeper in the stack, so projects like this are how I stay honest about front-end. Wrapping Fourier analysis of financial data in a UI I designed end-to-end was the right kind of uncomfortable.
