About The Author

About Michael Earl's Personal Interests

I'm a software engineer with a background primarily in backend systems. I built this project deliberately — as a way to get my hands dirty on the front-end side and own the full stack on something I actually cared about. I've always believed the best way to learn a new layer is to ship something real with it, even if it's rough at first. Failing fast on side projects so I don't fail slow at work is a principle I keep coming back to.

Outside of work I do ballroom dancing, play guitar, play soccer, and box. None of these am I particularly polished at — which is the point. Staying a beginner at things outside work keeps the learning muscle from atrophying. I'm also a committed OU football fan; it's my alma mater.

The Fourier transform has applications I find genuinely interesting, and financial data is a good playground for it. Wrapping that analysis in a front-end I built from scratch — choosing the layout, the interactions, the visual hierarchy — gave me the kind of end-to-end ownership that's hard to get on a backend team. That's the real reason this site exists.
